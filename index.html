A More Intentional Way Forward

Originally founded in 2014 and reimagined in 2026, Winter Green Coaching evolved from a passion for travel into a brand centered on clarity, growth, and meaningful journeys.

I help people reconnect with themselves, gain clarity, and move forward with purpose - whether through life coaching or intentional travel design.

My Story

My path to this work was not linear which is exactly what shaped it. I earned my Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, followed by a Master's degree in Clinical Psychology. My first job out of college was as a ski instructor in Vail, Colorado. Living in a new place and connecting with people from all walks of life was a defining experience. That experience led me to pursue graduate school in Santa Barbara, California, where I deepened my understanding of human behavior and personal growth.

A Season of Change

After working as a therapist, I made the intentional decision to stay home and raise my three children, who are now 26, 25 and 21. During that time, I launched my own business, Wintergreen Travel, and worked as a travel advisor serving the luxury market for 10 years. When Covid shifted the world, I closed that chapter. Now, 6 years later and an empty nester, I'm finding myself re-imagining my career path. I've combined my passion for helping others with my love of travel and with that, Winter Green Coaching was born. This work brings together personal growth and meaningful experiences to help my clients gain clarity and move forward with intention.

Life doesn't give only one right path - only one that feels right for you. My role is to uncover it, trust it, and move forward with clarity and confidence. Either as a coach or a travel expert.

Today, I live in Chicago with my husband, Mike of 28 years, and our 2 beloved dogs, Tucker and Meadow.
